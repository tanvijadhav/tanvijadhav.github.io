About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the CS Theory Group at Rutgers University, advised by Sumegha Garg. My research interests are in machine learning theory, complexity theory, learning theory and information theory.

Previously, I was a Research and Development Engineer at UC San Diego, advised by Dinesh Bharadia. Before that I was a Systems Engineer at Kumu Networks.

I graduated with an M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, advised by S. Sandeep Pradhan and a B.E.(Hons.) in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from BITS Pilani, K. K. Birla Goa Campus, India.
